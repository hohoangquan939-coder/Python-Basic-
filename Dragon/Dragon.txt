Dragon Information

Name: Dark Dragon
Type: Legendary Creature
Element: Fire and Shadow
Age: 500 years old
Strength: Extremely powerful
Ability: Can control flames and create massive explosions
Habitat: Ancient mountains and dark caves
Personality: Fierce, intelligent, and independent

Description:
The Dark Dragon is a legendary creature known for its incredible power and mysterious origins. 
It protects ancient lands and uses its fire abilities to defeat enemies. 
Although it looks dangerous, it is a wise creature that only attacks when threatened.
